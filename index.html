About

I am an architectural designer with over ten years of professional experience in architectural design, project coordination, and design management. Before moving to Vancouver, I worked in Shanghai with Atkins, Nikken Sekkei, and AECOM, contributing to architectural projects from design development to project coordination and client-facing communication.

My academic background includes a Bachelor of Architecture from Nanjing Tech University, an MA in Architectural Design from the University of Sheffield, and current graduate studies in High Performance Buildings at the University of British Columbia. My current interests focus on sustainable retrofit, building performance, energy modeling, façade renewal, and the transformation of existing buildings.
