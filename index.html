About

I have a background in architectural design, with a Bachelor of Architecture from Nanjing Tech University and an MA in Architectural Design from the University of Sheffield. I am currently studying High Performance Buildings at the University of British Columbia, where my work focuses on building retrofit, energy performance, façade strategies, and sustainable design.

My current interests include existing building renewal, energy modeling, sustainable retrofit, and the connection between architectural design and building performance.
